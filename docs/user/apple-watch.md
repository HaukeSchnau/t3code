# Apple Watch

The T3 Code watch app shows which agents need you, are working, or finished recently, across every environment your iPhone has saved. Open a thread to read where the agent stands, answer its question, tell it what to do next, or stop it.

The watch app installs with the iPhone app. Open T3 Code on the iPhone once after installing or updating, then open T3 Code on the watch. The watch talks to your environments through the iPhone, so keep the iPhone nearby; it doesn't need to be unlocked or have the app open.

To answer a question, tap one of its options or reply in your own words with dictation or Scribble. Replies to finished or failed work, and the quick replies, become the agent's next message. Approvals and questions with several parts still need the iPhone.

If the iPhone is out of reach when you reply, the reply waits and goes out once it's back. A reply that fails shows a **Reply not delivered** notification on the iPhone and the watch.
